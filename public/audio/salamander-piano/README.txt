VocalCut Studio retained recorded velocity layers 7 and 12 and the harmL
key-release resonances at roots MIDI 36 through 78 in minor thirds. The
stereo samples were converted from FLAC to MP3 and the note-on files were
limited to twelve seconds with a fade near the end where necessary.
